문항 1

I could see the fireworks from the roof of my building and take pictures of them, hoping to capture the moment and sharing it with my friends later.

sharing→to share

바른문장 I could see the fireworks from the roof of my building and take pictures of them, hoping to capture the moment and to share it with my friends later.

해석 나는 건물 옥상에서 불꽃놀이를 볼 수 있었고 그것들의 사진을 찍을 수 있었다, 그 순간을 포착하고 나중에 친구들과 공유하기를 바라면서.

문항 3

informing→informed

바른문장 The groups of workers accepted the new policy, asked the company to change it, or refused it, and later informed their coworkers about the outcome.

해석 그 근로자 집단들은 새로운 정책을 받아들이거나, 회사에 그것을 바꿔달라고 요청하거나, 그것을 거부했다, 그리고 나중에 동료들에게 결과에 대해 알렸다.

문항 4

Brown lines run along the backs of the amazing lizards and continue down their tails, making their skin pattern quite different from it of most other reptiles.

from it of→from that of

바른문장 Brown lines run along the backs of the amazing lizards and continue down their tails, making their skin pattern quite different from that of most other reptiles.

해석 갈색 줄무늬는 그 놀라운 도마뱀들의 등을 따라 이어지고 꼬리까지 계속된다, 그들의 피부 무늬를 대부분의 다른 파충류의 것과는 상당히 다르게 만들면서.

it/them vs. that/those (Chapter 11 POINT 3)

문항 6

Some children do not speak often but express themselves clearly when they do, despite they sometimes struggle to find the right words at first.

despite→although

바른문장 Some children do not speak often but express themselves clearly when they do, although they sometimes struggle to find the right words at first.

해석 어떤 아이들은 자주 말하지 않지만 말할 때는 자신을 명확하게 표현한다, 비록 그들이 처음에는 적절한 단어를 찾는 데 종종 어려움을 겪지만.

부사절 접속사 vs. 전치사 (Chapter 09 POINT 1)

바른문장 Serene quickly turned on one foot in front of her friends but fell to the floor, and her friends laughed loudly at the surprising fall.
