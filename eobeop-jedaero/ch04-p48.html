문항 1

Our rooms aren't ready, but the hotel will call us when they are, since the manager requested that guests waits in the lobby during peak hours.

waits→wait

바른문장 Our rooms aren't ready, but the hotel will call us when they are, since the manager requested that guests wait in the lobby during peak hours.

해석 우리 방은 아직 준비되지 않았지만, 준비되면 호텔에서 우리에게 전화를 줄 것이다, 매니저가 성수기 동안 손님들이 로비에서 기다려야 한다고 요청했기 때문이다.

주장·제안·요구·명령 동사 뒤 that절의 should 생략 (Chapter 04 POINT 2)

해설 request처럼 요청을 나타내는 동사 뒤 that절이 당위성을 나타낼 때는 동사가 「(should) + 동사원형」이 되어야 한다. 따라서 waits가 아니라 wait이 되어야 한다.

그래머콕 개념당위의 should 생략 (insist·suggest that)

문항 2

It was impossible to miss the $20 bill on the table. Surely you must have seen it! The bill was one of several items that was left on the table by mistake.

바른문장 It was impossible to miss the $20 bill on the table. Surely you must have seen it! The bill was one of several items that were left on the table by mistake.

문항 3

I did really enjoy exploring new hiking trails in the mountains during the weekends, and I must returned to that same trail at least five times last summer.

must returned→must have returned

바른문장 I did really enjoy exploring new hiking trails in the mountains during the weekends, and I must have returned to that same trail at least five times last summer.

해석 나는 주말마다 산속의 새로운 등산로를 탐험하는 것을 정말로 즐겼다, 그리고 지난여름 나는 분명 그 똑같은 등산로에 적어도 다섯 번은 다시 갔을 것이다.

문항 4

Some people urge that the government invest more funds in education than they do now. So great this need is that many teachers work extra unpaid hours.

this need is→is this need

바른문장 Some people urge that the government invest more funds in education than they do now. So great is this need that many teachers work extra unpaid hours.

해석 어떤 사람들은 정부가 지금보다 교육에 더 많은 예산을 투자해야 한다고 촉구한다. 이 필요성이 너무나 커서 많은 교사들이 무급으로 초과 근무를 한다.

문항 5

All travelers should bring their passports when they go to the airport, and most experienced tourists have too, so lines move quickly at security.

have→do

바른문장 All travelers should bring their passports when they go to the airport, and most experienced tourists do too, so lines move quickly at security.

해석 모든 여행객들은 공항에 갈 때 여권을 챙겨야 한다, 그리고 대부분의 노련한 관광객들도 그러하며, 그래서 보안 검색대에서 줄이 빠르게 움직인다.

반복되는 동사 대신 쓰는 do/be/have동사 (Chapter 04 POINT 3)

해설 앞에 나온 일반동사 bring을 대신하려면 have동사가 아니라 do동사를 써야 한다. 따라서 have가 아니라 do가 되어야 한다.

문항 6

Computers process information quickly, just like our brains do when we solve problems, and calculate results and storing data efficiently as well.

바른문장 Computers process information quickly, just like our brains do when we solve problems, and calculate results and store data efficiently as well.

해석 컴퓨터는 정보를 빠르게 처리하는데, 이는 우리가 문제를 풀 때 우리의 뇌가 하는 것과 같다, 그리고 컴퓨터는 결과를 계산하고 데이터를 효율적으로 저장하기도 한다.

문항 7

Susan could have taken the bus this morning, but she walked to work instead, even though she did complained about the distance later that day.

did complained→did complain

바른문장 Susan could have taken the bus this morning, but she walked to work instead, even though she did complain about the distance later that day.

해석 Susan은 오늘 아침 버스를 탈 수도 있었지만, 대신 걸어서 출근했다, 비록 그녀가 그날 늦게 거리에 대해 정말로 불평했지만.

문항 8

바른문장 I do have a key to the office right now, but it's downstairs in my car, and if I need it later, I could just walk down and get it.
