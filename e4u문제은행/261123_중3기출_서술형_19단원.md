## 762번

(D) Onions <u>used</u> to get rid of coughs are considered as natural remedies.
(E) The largest onion on record <u>did weighed</u> over 10 pounds!

(2)_________________________ → _________________________

The meaning of the word shampoo changed a few times after it first entered English around 1762. In the 19th century, shampoo got its present meaning of “washing the hair.” Shortly after that, the word began to be also using for a special soap for the hair.
