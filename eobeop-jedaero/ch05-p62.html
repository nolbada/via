문항 1

If someone were able to read other people's minds, would he or she still get along with friends and family? Absolutely not. If people knew too much about other people's thoughts, their relationships may become difficult. In fact, people would have understood each other better if humans developed this ability sooner.

바른문장 If someone were able to read other people's minds, would he or she still get along with friends and family? Absolutely not. If people knew too much about other people's thoughts, their relationships may become difficult. In fact, people would have understood each other better if humans had developed this ability sooner.

해석 만약 누군가가 다른 사람의 마음을 읽을 수 있다면, 그 사람은 여전히 친구와 가족과 잘 지낼 수 있을까? 결코 그렇지 않을 것이다. 만약 사람들이 다른 사람들의 생각에 대해 너무 많이 안다면, 그들의 관계는 어려워질 수도 있다. 실제로, 만약 인류가 이 능력을 더 일찍 발달시켰더라면 사람들은 서로를 더 잘 이해했을 것이다.

문항 2

If you look closely at the history of the world, you will find some inventions that have changed history forever. For example, the result of World War II might have been different if the atomic bomb had not been developed. Scientists devoted years to developing weapons that they hoped would end the war quickly, but few were prepared to accepting the consequences.

to accepting→to accept

바른문장 If you look closely at the history of the world, you will find some inventions that have changed history forever. For example, the result of World War II might have been different if the atomic bomb had not been developed. Scientists devoted years to developing weapons that they hoped would end the war quickly, but few were prepared to accept the consequences.

해석 만약 당신이 세계 역사를 자세히 살펴본다면, 역사를 영원히 바꾼 몇몇 발명품들을 발견하게 될 것이다. 예를 들어, 만약 원자폭탄이 개발되지 않았더라면 제2차 세계대전의 결과는 달랐을지도 모른다. 과학자들은 전쟁을 빨리 끝내기를 바랐던 무기를 개발하는 데 수년을 바쳤지만, 그 결과를 받아들일 준비가 된 사람은 거의 없었다.

전치사 to vs. to부정사의 to (Chapter 06 POINT 4)

해설 be prepared to는 전치사 to가 아니라 to부정사의 to를 취하는 표현이므로 뒤에는 동명사가 아니라 동사원형이 와야 한다. 따라서 to accepting이 아니라 to accept가 되어야 한다.

문항 3

Too many companies advertise their products as if they had no competitors. If there were no competitors in the market, those companies would survive, but that rarely happens in reality. Their marketing teams often act as if the competition doesn't exist at all.

doesn't exist→didn't exist

바른문장 Too many companies advertise their products as if they had no competitors. If there were no competitors in the market, those companies would survive, but that rarely happens in reality. Their marketing teams often act as if the competition didn't exist at all.

해석 너무 많은 회사들이 마치 경쟁자가 없는 것처럼 자사 제품을 광고한다. 만약 시장에 경쟁자가 없다면, 그 회사들은 살아남을 것이다, 하지만 그런 일은 현실에서 거의 일어나지 않는다. 그들의 마케팅팀은 종종 마치 경쟁이 전혀 존재하지 않는 것처럼 행동한다.

문항 4

If Amy hadn't avoided last week's interview because of her fear of failure, she might have gotten her dream job. You will achieve much more in life if you accept that failure is sometimes normal. This is a lesson especially important for people which struggle with the fear of failure.

바른문장 If Amy hadn't avoided last week's interview because of her fear of failure, she might have gotten her dream job. You will achieve much more in life if you accept that failure is sometimes normal. This is a lesson especially important for people who struggle with the fear of failure.
