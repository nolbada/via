문항 1

Children are more capable of learning multiple languages or adapting to new environments than adults, so many parents enjoy to watch their kids pick up new words so quickly.

enjoy to watch→enjoy watching

바른문장 Children are more capable of learning multiple languages or adapting to new environments than adults, so many parents enjoy watching their kids pick up new words so quickly.

해석 아이들은 어른들보다 여러 언어를 배우거나 새로운 환경에 적응하는 능력이 더 뛰어나다, 그래서 많은 부모들은 아이들이 새로운 단어를 그렇게 빨리 습득하는 것을 지켜보는 것을 즐긴다.

동명사와 to부정사 중 하나만 목적어로 취하는 동사 (Chapter 06 POINT 2)

해설 enjoy는 목적어로 to부정사가 아니라 동명사만을 취하는 동사이다. 따라서 to watch가 아니라 watching이 되어야 한다.

  2. 동명사만 목적어로 받는 동사: enjoy, avoid, finish, mind
  3. pick up = (단어 등을) 익히다
문항 2

바른문장 When you first meet a German host, he will shake hands, greet everyone standing there, and even bow his head slightly, and offer a small gift as a sign of respect.

문항 4

After a long day at work, Sally goes for a run to clear her mind or takes a warm bath, since exercise makes her feel as better as a relaxing bath does.

as better as→as good as

바른문장 After a long day at work, Sally goes for a run to clear her mind or takes a warm bath, since exercise makes her feel as good as a relaxing bath does.

해석 퇴근 후 힘든 하루를 보낸 뒤, Sally는 마음을 정리하기 위해 달리기를 하거나 따뜻한 목욕을 한다, 운동이 편안한 목욕만큼이나 그녀를 기분 좋게 만들어주기 때문에.
