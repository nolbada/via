문항 1

have recently launched→has recently launched

바른문장 The company is having difficulties because of the highly competitive situation in the market, especially since one of its biggest competitors has recently launched a similar product.

해석 그 회사는 시장의 치열한 경쟁 상황 때문에 어려움을 겪고 있다, 특히 가장 큰 경쟁사 중 하나가 최근 비슷한 제품을 출시했기 때문에.

바른문장 The patient, as the doctor emphasized, must have meals and take medicine at fixed times, though he sometimes forgets to take his medicine on time.

동사 vs. 동명사/to부정사 (Chapter 06 POINT 1)
